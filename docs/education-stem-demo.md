# Education & STEM Demo

The `education-stem-demo` template is a flagship featured workspace for curiosity-driven learning, guided exploration, project work, and durable understanding.

It is designed to show how a repository can become a learning environment instead of just a folder of files.

## Purpose

This demo exists to provide a complete out-of-the-box experience for:

- Analytics & Reporting
- Education Knowledge Pack
- Analytics Sample Data Pack
- Publishing & TeX
- AI-assisted learning
- repository-first onboarding
- reproducible projects

The goal is to give students, teachers, parents, mentors, workshop organizers, and self-learners something they can open and explore immediately.

## Learning Story

## Repository-First Onboarding

The demo is meant to teach the platform through the repository itself:

```text
Repository
    ↓
Workspace Discovery
    ↓
Provision Environment
    ↓
Read Documentation
    ↓
Start Working
```

Start with:

- `README.md`
- `docs/learning-path.md`
- `docs/projects.md`
- `docs/example-prompts.md`
- `docs/educator-guide.md` or `docs/parent-guide.md`
